## 🏛️ What I build

**[islam.museum](https://github.com/amado2k5/islam-museum)** — a digital museum of Islamic history and an open knowledge graph, built by one person. More than 80,000 structured, source-cited records (people, places, events, books, objects) published as a quarter of a million pages in Arabic, English and Urdu, with French and Spanish in progress. The dataset is CC0 and archived on Zenodo ([concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23692511](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23692511)); a peer-style whitepaper describes its method and validation state ([DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24273662](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24273662)). It includes a kids' wing, an education explorer mapped to Ontario's curriculum, a public API, and a storytelling podcast for children. Free for everyone, personally funded, no tracking — and preserved by Library and Archives Canada. On the [Tranco](https://tranco-list.eu/) ranking of the world's domains, it climbed from **#4,609,967 on 23 October 2026** (its first appearance, twelve days after launch) to **#887,932 on 19 November 2026** — a rise of more than 3.7 million places in 27 days, entering the top million five weeks after launch.

**Fifi Recipes** — my late mother's Egyptian home cooking, kept alive as a cookbook that computes. [fifi.cooking](https://fifi.cooking) plus six native apps from one codebase family: [Android](https://github.com/amado2k5/fifirecipes-android), [iPhone & iPad](https://github.com/amado2k5/fifirecipes-ipadosapp), [tvOS](https://github.com/amado2k5/fifirecipes-tvos), [Fire TV](https://github.com/amado2k5/fifirecipes-amazonfire) and [Samsung TV](https://github.com/amado2k5/fifirecipe-samsungsmarttv). Live on the App Store and Google Play.

**[Cookwala](https://github.com/amado2k5/cookwala)** — an open standard for cooking safely, written for people, kitchens and robots: a core specification, schemas and conformance vectors, with [Homebrew](https://github.com/amado2k5/homebrew-cookwala) and [Scoop](https://github.com/amado2k5/scoop-cookwala) distribution.

**[Origins](https://github.com/amado2k5/faith-origins)** — the works of Dr Hamdy Abdel Aal, presented in Arabic and English.

**[History Navigator](https://github.com/amado2k5/historynavigator)** — the interactive timeline prototype, built with Google AI Studio in 2025, from which the museum grew.

Earlier experiments include [costimator](https://github.com/amado2k5/costimator) and the [what-if visualizer](https://github.com/amado2k5/whatif-visualizer).

## 🤖 How I work with AI

- **One-person studio, agent workforce.** I plan and decide; AI agents (Muse, Devin, Gemini and others) research, draft, translate, code and check — under a written decision ledger, not vibes.
- **Gates, not trust.** Every change passes mechanical validation and citation checks before it ships; claims in public copy are reconciled against the project's own records.
- **Honest by default.** AI involvement is disclosed wherever a platform or a form asks, and in the projects' own pages.
